# 📊 Social Media Ads Campaigns Analysis (Power BI)

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of multiple **social media advertising campaigns** using **Power BI**.  The goal of this report is to evaluate campaign performance, 
identify the most cost-efficient ads, understand audience reach, and highlight key metrics like impressions, click rate, conversion rate, and spend efficiency.

---

## 📁 Project Overview

This analysis covers **three major ad campaigns** (Campaign 916, 936, and 1178) and compares their performance across:

- Impressions & Reach  
- Click Rates  
- Average Cost per Click (CPC)  
- Conversions & Sales  
- Gender-based Reach  
- Age Group Targeting  
- Conversion Success Ratio (Approved vs Total Conversions)

The final Power BI dashboard provides a **deep comparative view** of campaign effectiveness and audience insights.

---

## 🎯 Key Insights

### 🔹 **1. Click Rate Comparison**
- Campaign **936** has the highest Click Rate (0.077)  
- Campaign **1178**: 0.031  
- Campaign **916**: 0.010  
➡️ *Campaign 936 performs best in terms of user engagement.*

### 🔹 **2. Average Cost Per Click (CPC)**
- Campaign 1178: **$1.59**  
- Campaign 936: **$0.70**  
- Campaign 916: **$0.69**  
➡️ *Campaign 936 and 916 are the most cost-efficient.*

### 🔹 **3. Impressions & Reach**
- Campaign **1178** accounts for **32%** of ads but generates **89% of total impressions**  
➡️ *Best campaign in terms of overall reach and visibility.*

### 🔹 **4. Targeted Age Group**
- All campaigns mostly target the **30–34 age group**  
➡️ *Audience concentration is strongest in this segment.*

### 🔹 **5. Conversion Success Ratio (Approved vs Total Conversions)**  
Success Ratios:  
- Campaign 916 → **0.42**  
- Campaign 936 → **0.93**  
- Campaign 1178 → **0.93**  
➡️ *Campaign 936 and 1178 show the highest conversion efficiency.*

### 🔹 **6. Average Clicks**
- Campaign 916 → 2  
- Campaign 936 → 4  
- Campaign 1178 → 4  
➡️ *Campaign 936 & 1178 attract more clicks per ad.*

### 🔹 **7. Gender-Based Reach**
- Campaign 916 → 65% Male / 35% Female  
- Campaign 936 → 55% Male / 45% Female  
- Campaign 1178 → 100% Male  
➡️ *Campaign 936 is the most gender-balanced.*
